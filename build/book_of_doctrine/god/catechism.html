Catechism

Introduction
0 The Deist Catechism is an accessible summary of Deist thought. 1 It serves children and adults, instructing them in what is right, and insulating them from what is wrong. 2 The Deist Catechism exists to edify the intellect, inspire ethical living, and cultivate a sense of awe and wonder towards the universe.
Catechism
0 What is the chief end of humankind?
1 The chief end of humankind is to find God
2 Hasn't God been found already in the form of Yahweh, Jesus, and Allah?
3 No, as these are false Gods. 4 No one has ever heard, nor seen them
5 Where is God?
6 God is outside of this universe
7 Can you see God?
8 Not at this time
9 Does God hear our prayers?
10 No. God does not hear us
11 Should you glorify God?
12 Sure, if you would like
13 How much faith should I put in the testaments three?
14 The first two testaments represent humankind's struggle for truth. The third represents our modern understanding. All are fallible, and the first two are errant.
15 Who were our first parents?
16 Chromosomal Adam and Eve, from Africa
17 Of what were our first parents made?
18 They were the continuation of an earlier, similar species
19 What is a covenant?
20 An agreement between two or more persons.
21 How many Gods are there?
22 One. The Creator God
23 Does God know all things?
24 No
25 Who wrote the Bible?
26 Ordinary people
27 Should I live my life according to the first two testaments?
28 No, as they are legacy. A better life doctrine is the Third Testament, which has superseded the first two
29 What is the purpose of religion?
30 The purpose of religion is to empower the people socially
31 Am I not then equally served by attending a traditional house of worship?
32 If you appreciate traditional doctrine, this is the better path for you
33 What is the Beatific Vision?
34 The Beatific Vision will occur when humankind unequivocally witnesses the face of God
35 How will we know this event has occurred?
36 You will investigate the claims scientifically
37 What is the Ascension?
38 The Ascension is the period after the Beatific Vision in which we become communicant with God
