Design Principles

0 Design principles of Book of Julian include:
  1. Clarity
    1. Hierarchical Organization
    2. Images
    3. Diagrams
    4. Metaphors
    5. Examples
  2. Simplicity
  3. Brevity
  4. Accuracy
  5. Practical Value
1 When these principles are in conflict, simple expressions are preferred.
