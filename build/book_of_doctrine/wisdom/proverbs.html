1 Where there is strife, there is pride, but wisdom is found in those who take advice860 .
2 Even fools are thought wise if they keep silent861 .
3 For the living know that they will die, but the dead know nothing; they have no further reward, and even their name is forgotten862 .

7 One who has unreliable friends soon comes to ruin, but there is a friend who sticks closer than a brother866 .

9 A generous person will prosper; whoever refreshes others will be refreshed868 .
